Hyperion Tech Hub
Business Plan
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-01-30

1. Executive Summary
Hyperion Tech Hub is a physical technology center designed to provide education, IT services, and enterprise-grade software solutions. The hub will serve schools, businesses, and individuals through training programs, printing services, repairs, and software development. The business will generate income from a diverse mix of service lines, lowering risk and enabling steady growth.

2. Company Overview
Name: Hyperion Tech Hub
Business Model: Service, training, and software delivery hub
Location: Abuja, Nigeria
Website: https://www.hyperiontechhub.com/

Mission:
Make technology accessible and impactful for Nigerian schools and SMEs through systems, training, and retainers.

Vision:
Become Abuja’s trusted digital partner for schools and growing SMEs — systems we build, host, train on, and support.

3. Services and Revenue Streams
Primary offers (public brand pillars):
- Hyperion EduSuite (School OS) — setup + term/annual SaaS
- SME Digital Business Kit — Presence / Commerce / Ops fixed SKUs
- Hyperion Care — managed IT/cyber retainers (monthly)
- Corporate & career training (hub + LMS)

Supporting / cash-side (not brand identity):
- Computer and laptop repairs
- Graphic design and printing
- Custom software when kits are not enough

Revenue streams:
- Training fees (in-person and online cohorts)
- Printing and photocopying (walk-in)
- Repairs and maintenance (walk-in)
- Software / kit project fees
- School system subscriptions (EduSuite)
- Hyperion Care retainers
- Design services

4. Market Analysis
Target customers:
- Schools and educational institutions
- SMEs and local businesses
- Students and job seekers
- General public for printing and repairs

Market demand:
- Digital transformation in schools and SMEs
- Continuous demand for printing, repairs, and training
- Growing need for cloud and cybersecurity support

Competitive advantage:
- Multi-service portfolio
- Ability to deliver and maintain full systems
- Training and delivery under one roof

5. Marketing and Sales Strategy
Channels:
- Partnerships with schools and institutions
- Social media and local advertising
- Community outreach and workshops
- Referrals and client testimonials

Sales approach:
- Bundle printing with training
- Offer pilot programs for school systems
- Provide recurring support packages

6. Operations Plan
Facility:
- Training room and computer lab
- Printing and service desk
- Software development workspace

Staffing (phase 1):
- Hub Manager
- 2 to 3 Trainers
- Front Desk and Print Officer
- Software Developer
- Hardware Technician
- IT Support and Network Admin

Core systems:
- Billing and enrollment tracking
- Service ticketing for repairs
- Project management for software delivery

7. Technology Plan
Key assets:
- 15 to 30 workstations
- Printers, scanners, and photocopier
- Backup power (generator/inverter/solar)
- Secure network and data management

8. Financial Plan
Startup cost (estimate): NGN 12,000,000
Monthly revenue target at maturity: NGN 4,350,000
Monthly net profit at maturity: NGN 998,000
Break-even: Months 15 to 17

Financial projections are detailed in the financial model file.

9. Milestones
Month 1 to 2:
- Location secured and outfitted
- Services launch: printing, repairs, basic training

Month 3 to 6:
- Launch school portal pilots
- Start corporate training contracts

Month 6 to 12:
- Expand training capacity
- Add advanced cybersecurity and cloud services

10. Risks and Mitigation
Risk: Power instability
Mitigation: Backup power and surge protection

Risk: Low early enrollment
Mitigation: Partnerships and promotional cohorts

Risk: Project delivery delays
Mitigation: Agile delivery and milestone payments

11. Conclusion
Hyperion Tech Hub is a viable, scalable venture with a strong market need and a diversified service portfolio. The phased rollout and recurring revenue streams support sustainable growth.
