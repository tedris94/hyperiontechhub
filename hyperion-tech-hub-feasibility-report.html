1. Executive Summary

Hyperion Tech Hub intends to establish a physical office and training center that provides technology services, education, and enterprise software solutions. The hub will serve individuals, schools, and businesses with a mix of walk-in services, structured training programs, and contracted software delivery.

2. Project Description and Objectives

Project: Establish a physical Hyperion Tech Hub facility.

Objectives:

  • Provide a one-stop destination for tech training, repairs, printing, and software solutions.
  • Build recurring revenue from schools and corporate customers.
  • Develop in-house capacity for web, mobile, and enterprise systems.

3. Services Scope

  • AI and Native Coding
  • WordPress and Web Development
  • Cloud Services
  • Android Development
  • Corporate Training
  • Online Courses
  • Computer and Laptop Repairs
  • Graphic Design and Printing
  • Cybersecurity
  • Computer Studies Center
  • School Portal System
  • Student Management System
  • Result System
  • Custom App Development (Web, Desktop, Mobile)

4. Market Feasibility

Demand drivers include digital transformation in schools and SMEs, consistent demand for printing and repairs, and increased need for cybersecurity and cloud support.

5. Technical Feasibility

Key requirements include 15 to 30 workstations, reliable internet, print equipment, backup power, and secure networking.

6. Operational Feasibility

Phase 1 staffing includes a hub manager, trainers, front desk officer, developer, technician, and IT support.

7. Legal and Regulatory Feasibility

Compliance areas include business registration, licensing, and data protection for students and clients.

8. Financial Feasibility

Startup cost estimate: NGN 12,000,000. Monthly revenue target at maturity: NGN 4,350,000. Monthly net profit at maturity: NGN 998,000.

9. Risk Assessment and Mitigation

  • Power instability: Backup power and surge protection.
  • Low early enrollment: School partnerships and promotional cohorts.
  • Project delays: Agile delivery and milestone payments.

10. Implementation Plan

  • Months 1 to 2: Location setup, equipment, launch printing and training.
  • Months 3 to 6: Launch school portal pilots and software delivery.
  • Months 6 to 12: Scale training capacity and support contracts.

11. Conclusion and Recommendation

The project is feasible with strong market demand, a diversified revenue structure, and a phased implementation plan that reduces risk.
