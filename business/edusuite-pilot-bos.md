# EduSuite pilot — Bright Olivelight Schools

## Why this school

- Existing term-subscription relationship (validated willingness to pay)
- Live portal already in production under Hyperion support
- Strongest path to a public EduSuite flagship case study

## Pilot scope (MVP)

1. Student & parent accounts  
2. Fees + Paystack (or bridge existing payment process)  
3. Results / report cards  
4. Attendance  
5. Admin notices  

## Steps

1. **Permission** — Written approval for case study + testimonial.  
2. **Discovery** — Map current School Management Pro / Educare fields and term calendar.  
3. **Staging** — Twin results + fees on EduSuite staging; keep live WP until cutover.  
4. **Training** — Half-day session for admin (hub or on-site) + LMS checklist.  
5. **Soft launch** — One term parallel run.  
6. **Cutover** — Switch parents to EduSuite URLs; attach Hyperion Care Growth.  
7. **Publish** — Update `/portfolio/bright-olivelight-schools` with outcomes.

## Commercial

- Setup within EduSuite band (₦500k–₦2.5M) — credit prior work where fair  
- Per-term SaaS or annual prepaid  
- Care Growth ₦150k/month after go-live  

## Success criteria

- Admin runs fees/results without developer tickets for routine tasks  
- At least one parent pays or views results online successfully  
- Written testimonial received  
- Care retainer signed or scheduled
