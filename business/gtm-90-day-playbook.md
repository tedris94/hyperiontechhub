Execute this playbook to turn the portfolio plan into pipeline. Do not expand into clinic/government products until Tier-1 pipeline is full.

| # | Action | Owner | Done when |

## Days 31–60 — Sell the wedge

### School outreach (target: 20 demos)

Use `outreach/school-demo-email.md`. Channels:
- Kubwa / Bwari / Abuja private & Islamic school directories  
- Parent/principal WhatsApp intros  
- Visits from the physical hub  

Track in a simple sheet: School | Contact | Demo date | Outcome | Next step.

### SME proposals (target: 10)

Verticals: water/FMCG, retail, clinics, estates. Use SME Kit templates. Always attach Care.

### Training cohort (1 paid)

- Title: **Web Development Fundamentals — Abuja Cohort**  
- Duration: 6 weeks · Price: ₦85,000  
- Delivery: Hub + LMS (`/courses`)  
- Enrolment CTA on site + flyers at hub  

See `outreach/training-cohort-brief.md`.

### Site

Ensure EduSuite, SME Kit, Care, Schools, SMEs, Portfolio, honest metrics are live.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Mon:** Pipeline review (schools + SMEs + retainers)  
- **Wed:** Demo / proposal day  
- **Fri:** Case study / content + Care follow-ups  

## Success metrics (90 days)

- ≥ 20 school demo conversations  
- ≥ 10 SME Kit proposals  
- ≥ 1 paid training cohort launched  
- ≥ 3 named case studies public  
- ≥ 3 Care retainers signed or in negotiation
