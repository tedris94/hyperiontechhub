# Hyperion EduSuite — MVP Definition & Pricing

**Status:** Active product definition  
**Owner:** Hyperion Tech Hub  
**Pilot school:** Bright Olivelight Schools (recommended)

## Positioning

School operating system for Nigerian private and Islamic schools. Sold as **setup + per-term / annual SaaS**, not a one-off website.

## MVP modules (must ship first)

1. **Student & parent accounts** — admin, teacher, parent, student roles  
2. **Fees & Paystack payments** — fee structures, invoices, online pay, reconciliation  
3. **Results & report cards** — term scores, printable reports, parent visibility  
4. **Attendance** — daily/class attendance with export  
5. **Admin dashboard & notices** — announcements via email/SMS hooks  

Later modules (post-MVP): hostel, transport, LMS link, payroll, inventory.

## Pricing (NGN)

| Line | Range | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Setup (one-time) | ₦500,000 – ₦2,500,000 | Campus size, migration, SMS |
| Per-term SaaS | ₦150,000 – ₦450,000 | By student band |
| Annual SaaS | ₦400,000 – ₦1,200,000 | Prefer annual for cashflow |

Bundle **Hyperion Care Starter** for year one when annual is prepaid.

## Pilot plan — Bright Olivelight Schools

1. Map current School Management / Educare workflows and term calendar.  
2. Twin critical paths (results + fees) on EduSuite staging.  
3. Train admin staff (half-day hub session + LMS checklist).  
4. Soft launch one term; keep WordPress fallback if needed.  
5. Collect testimonial + publish case study (with permission).  
6. Attach Hyperion Care Growth at go-live.

## Sales talk track

> “We already run school portals on term subscriptions. EduSuite is the productised version — fees, results, attendance, parents — with Abuja support and a clear monthly/term fee.”
