# Module Prompt: Parent/Guardian Portal (Hyperion EduSuite)

Design the **Parent/Guardian Portal** for **Hyperion EduSuite**. Use brand colors: Primary `#1A2BC2`, Navy `#0D0D52`, Charcoal `#1B1C1E`, Black `#000000`, White `#FFFFFF`. See `BRAND-COLORS-REFERENCE.md`.

## Scope

- **Children overview**: Cards or list of linked children (name, class, photo). Select one to see detail.
- **Performance**: Grades by term, subject-wise marks, report card download (PDF button).
- **Attendance**: Calendar or list view of present/absent. Summary (days present, absent, leave).
- **Fees**: Outstanding amount, due date. List of invoices; pay button; payment history. Download receipt.
- **Messages**: Inbox from school (announcements, alerts). Compose reply if needed.
- **Meetings**: Request meeting (date, time, subject). List of scheduled/past meetings.
- **Documents**: Downloaded report cards, certificates, letters.

## UX

- Simple nav (Overview, Performance, Attendance, Fees, Messages, Meetings). Child switcher at top when multiple children. Mobile-friendly cards and lists.

## Deliverables

- Children overview and child-detail layout.
- Performance + report card download.
- Attendance summary and calendar/list.
- Fee summary, invoice list, payment history.
- Messages inbox and meeting request form.
- Empty and loading states.
- Desktop and mobile.
