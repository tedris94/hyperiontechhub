## URLs (local)

- Hub: http://localhost:3000/icms
- Public: http://localhost:3000/icms/anas-bn-malik
- Admin: http://localhost:3000/icms/admin/anas-bn-malik

## Narrative (≈10 minutes)

1. **Open hub** (`/icms`) — “Hyperion ICMS is multi-tenant. Anas bn Malik is Tenant #1.”
2. **Public Home** — brand-first hero, motto, Donate + Prayer Times CTAs. Scroll: prayer strip → events → Waqf → articles → Abuja contact.
3. **Mosque** — weekly timetable story (ops credibility).
4. **Donate** — pick amount/fund, show demo confirmation (trust + future Paystack).
5. **Leadership + Shurah/Committee + Articles** — institutional directory, HR/Shurah roster, and CMS content.
6. **Admin Dashboard** — KPIs, prayer strip, recent donations/events.
7. **Articles CMS** — table + editor: “non-technical staff can publish.”
8. **Settings** — logo, colors, domain label: white-label proof.
9. **Close** — “Same platform onboards the next Islamic center without rebuilding.”

- Emerald / gold / ivory brand identity applied as tenant theme tokens
