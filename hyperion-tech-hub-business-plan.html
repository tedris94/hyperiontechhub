Hyperion Tech Hub

Business Plan

Version: 1.0

Date: 2026-01-30

Website: https://www.hyperiontechhub.com/

1. Executive Summary

Hyperion Tech Hub is a physical technology center designed to provide education, IT services, and enterprise software solutions. The hub will serve schools, businesses, and individuals through training programs, printing services, repairs, and software development.

2. Company Overview

Mission: Make technology accessible, understandable, and impactful.

Vision: Become the trusted technology partner for schools and SMEs.

3. Services and Revenue Streams

  • AI and Native Coding
  • WordPress and Web Development
  • Cloud Services
  • Android Development
  • Corporate Training
  • Online Courses
  • Computer and Laptop Repairs
  • Graphic Design and Printing
  • Cybersecurity
  • Computer Studies Center
  • School Portal System
  • Student Management System
  • Result System
  • Custom App Development (Web, Desktop, Mobile)

4. Market Analysis

Demand is driven by digital transformation in schools and SMEs, ongoing need for printing and repair services, and increased adoption of cloud and cybersecurity services.

5. Marketing and Sales Strategy

  • School partnerships and pilot deployments
  • Community workshops and bootcamps
  • Social media and referral programs

6. Operations Plan

Phase 1 staffing includes a hub manager, trainers, front desk officer, developer, technician, and IT support.

7. Financial Plan

Startup cost estimate: NGN 12,000,000. Break-even: Months 15 to 17.

Detailed projections are available in the financial model file.

8. Risks and Mitigation

  • Power instability: Backup power and surge protection.
  • Low early enrollment: Partnerships and promotional cohorts.
  • Project delays: Agile delivery and milestone payments.

9. Conclusion

Hyperion Tech Hub is a viable, scalable venture with a strong market need and a diversified service portfolio. The phased rollout and recurring revenue streams support sustainable growth.
